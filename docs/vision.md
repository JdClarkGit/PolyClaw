# Vision

## What is PolyClaw?

PolyClaw is your personal AI trading assistant for Polymarket. It's designed to give individual traders the same intelligence capabilities that institutional traders have.

**The core idea:** Run your own trading intelligence system. Own your data. Make better decisions.

## Why PolyClaw?

### The Problem

Polymarket has become the most liquid prediction market in history. But:

- **Information asymmetry** — Whales and institutions have better tools
- **Data silos** — Trade data is scattered and hard to analyze
- **Manual tracking** — Following good traders is tedious
- **No alerts** — You miss trades while offline
- **Strategy opacity** — Hard to understand why traders succeed

### The Solution

PolyClaw solves this by giving you:

1. **Instant wallet analysis** — Understand any trader in seconds
2. **Real-time alerts** — Never miss a whale trade
3. **Strategy detection** — Learn what makes traders successful
4. **AI assistance** — Get personalized strategy advice
5. **Local-first** — Your data stays yours

## Core Philosophy

### 🏠 Local-First

PolyClaw runs on your machine. Your API keys, webhook URLs, and analysis data never leave your control.

**Why it matters:**
- Privacy — Your research stays private
- Speed — No network latency for local operations
- Reliability — Works offline for cached data
- Trust — Verify the code yourself

### 🔓 Open Source

PolyClaw is MIT licensed. You can:
- Read every line of code
- Modify it for your needs
- Build products on top of it
- Contribute improvements back

**Why it matters:**
- Transparency — No hidden data collection
- Customization — Adapt to your workflow
- Community — Benefit from collective improvements
- Longevity — Won't disappear if a company shuts down

### 🤖 AI-Augmented

PolyClaw uses AI to amplify your analysis, not replace your judgment.

**AI helps with:**
- Pattern recognition in large datasets
- Strategy articulation and comparison
- Generating hypotheses to test
- Explaining complex trading behavior

**You decide:**
- Which wallets to track
- What strategies to use
- When to trade
- How much to risk

### 📊 Data-Driven

Every feature is built around making better use of data:

- **Collect** — Pull comprehensive trade history
- **Analyze** — Calculate metrics that matter
- **Compare** — Benchmark against top performers
- **Act** — Get alerts and insights when it matters

## What PolyClaw Is Not

### ❌ Not a Trading Bot

PolyClaw doesn't execute trades. It provides intelligence to inform your decisions.

### ❌ Not Financial Advice

PolyClaw is a tool, not an advisor. All trading decisions are yours.

### ❌ Not a Guarantee

Past performance doesn't guarantee future results. Use PolyClaw as one input among many.
